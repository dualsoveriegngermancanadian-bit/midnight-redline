# Midnight Redline — Team-Owner Progression

The player can grow from a single-car operation into a complete professional drag-racing team. The progression is designed around capabilities that affect the race weekend, not decorative spending alone.

| Tier | Operation | Core unlocks | Competitive effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Privateer garage | One primary car, basic tools, one crew lead, and one active build. | Learn the build, dyno, and event loop. |
| 2 | Enclosed transport | A dedicated trailer, expanded parts storage, and a transport checklist. | Keep a second car or spare engine staged and reduce missed-service states. |
| 3 | Pro hauler | Fifth-wheel hauler, engine rack, tire station, dyno support, and a larger crew. | Perform engine swaps, tire service, and multi-car preparation between events. |
| 4 | Regional team | Multiple cars, multiple spare engines, specialist crew roles, and hospitality/control space. | Stage different performance bands and manage race-weekend logistics. |
| 5 | National operation | Full transport fleet, expanded inventory, advanced pit equipment, and rival-team paddock presence. | Maintain several race-ready builds and pursue the final host campaign at full scale. |

## Crew Roles

The pit crew is modeled as functional roles: crew chief validates the setup and signs off the build; engine specialist diagnoses failures and performs swaps; tuner runs the dyno and manages calibration; tire and chassis specialist manages grip and suspension setup; logistics lead checks spares, transport, and parts; and event manager handles the race-weekend board and readiness state.

## Purchase and Service Rules

Team assets may be represented as separate catalog products only when their capability, price, compatibility, and entitlement are defined. A hauler does not automatically grant a new car. A spare engine does not automatically install itself. The player sees the order status, service duration, crew action, resulting capability, and updated team inventory.

A failed engine moves a car to **ENGINE OUT**. A compatible, bank-confirmed replacement and a crew swap return it to **DYNO CHECK**, then **RACE READY** after validation. A second owned car can remain available if it is already race-ready. This creates a meaningful operational choice without forcing an unannounced purchase.

The active payment path remains RBC business e-transfer. The game records pending orders and verified entitlements but never stores bank credentials. Live collection remains disabled until the approved bank-side business enrollment and confirmation adapter are configured.
