# Asset Manifest: Fast & Furious — Midnight Redline

**Art direction:** Cinematic midnight street drag racing seen through 1990s Japanese automotive editorial design. The scene is a wet, blue-black industrial quarter-mile below concrete freeway spans, punctuated by warm sodium-vapor practical lights, brushed aluminium instrumentation, and reserved dry-vermilion performance cues. The perspective stays low and lateral: cars, start light, track surface, and finish truss remain legible at a glance.
