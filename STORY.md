# Fast & Furious: Midnight Redline — Campaign Brief

## Premise

The city’s long-closed **Harborline Testway** has reopened for one night only. A broker known as **Switchback** is running a six-race underground qualifier to identify the one driver capable of stopping the zero-day theft of a customs impound convoy. Each win pays for another engine part, another entry fee, and another piece of the broker’s route map. You are not chasing a crown; you are building enough power, trust, and track intelligence to reach the final redline event before the convoy moves at dawn.

This campaign is designed as an original, authorized Fast & Furious-branded experience. It relies on new events, rival drivers, cars, and story copy rather than reproducing a film scene or character likeness.

## Rival Tone

The rivals read as capable specialists, not disposable villains. **Nova** is the first honest benchmark and communicates the launch tutorial. **Kade** is a reaction-time technician who challenges the player’s staging discipline. **Marisol** tunes for imperfect conditions and exposes the value of grip. **Bishop** is a heavy-power gatekeeper who teaches gearing. **Torque** makes boost management the point of the event. **Switchback** is a data-driven final challenger whose car is built from the same upgrade logic available to the player.

## Race Loop

Each event begins with a concise pit-board briefing that gives one mechanical objective. The player pays an entry buy-in from earned cash, sets the engine tune, and stages at the tree. The race begins with the amber-to-green sequence. Throttle management governs launch quality; manual shift timing controls power-band retention; nitrous is a single deliberate decision. Results explain reaction time, elapsed time, trap speed, and the decisive performance delta. A win pays the purse, unlocks the next event, and enables the recommended garage module.

## Membership Proposition

**Nitro Pass** is presented as an optional seasonal garage membership. The first playable version treats it as a polished product surface rather than a live payment flow: it highlights a monthly event vault, premium livery drops, member-only time trials, seasonal garage signals, and additional replay telemetry. Competitive race power remains earned through ordinary campaign play so membership does not erase the mechanical core.

> “Stage clean. Shift mean. Make the night move.”
