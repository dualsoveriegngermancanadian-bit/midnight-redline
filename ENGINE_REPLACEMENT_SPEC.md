# Midnight Redline — Engine Replacement Specification

A blown engine is a service state, not a hidden punishment or an automatic charge. When a race or reliability event marks the installed engine as failed, the player’s build becomes **ENGINE OUT** and cannot enter another event until a compatible replacement is confirmed and installed.

Replacement engines are separate one-time products. Each engine has an archetype compatibility rule, horsepower, reliability rating, price, installation duration, and dyno result. The pit crew presents the failed-engine diagnosis, the available replacement choices, the full price, and the resulting build statistics before installation. A replacement is not installed from a client-side button alone; the bank-confirmed order must be reconciled first.

After a confirmed purchase, the pit crew swaps the failed unit, records the replacement engine ID and installation time, resets the engine reliability state, and returns the car to dyno-ready status. Existing compatible upgrades remain attached only where the replacement rules allow them; incompatible add-ons are clearly flagged for removal or re-selection. The dyno then reports the replacement engine’s actual horsepower and the complete build’s resulting torque estimate, grip, power-to-weight, and estimated ET.

The shop must not imply that a replacement is free, automatic, or covered by a prior purchase unless the applicable product explicitly says so. It must also distinguish real-world build context from customer-facing game prices. No engine replacement, subscription renewal, or race entry is granted without a matching confirmed payment record.
