# Midnight Redline — Build Validation Map

The garage treats add-ons as installed build components rather than decorative purchase labels. Mechanical parts modify the player build and are validated against the selected vehicle archetype. Cosmetic products are explicitly labeled as visual-only and do not claim to increase horsepower, grip, weight, or elapsed time.

| Component group | Products | Validated effect |
|---|---|---|
| Calibration | Dyno Street Tune; ECU Flash | Horsepower increase; ECU Flash is restricted to modern and exotic vehicles. |
| Contact | 10.5 Drag Radials | Grip increase and cleaner launch behavior. |
| Fuelling | 4-Barrel Carb Kit | Horsepower increase; heritage vehicles only. |
| Boost | Wet Nitrous Kit | Temporary horsepower effect for one 1.6-second shot. |
| Driveline | Short-Ratio Transmission; 4.10 Rear End | Horsepower and launch-grip changes; restricted to compatible archetypes. |
| Chassis | Weight Relief | Weight reduction that changes power-to-weight and acceleration. |
| Forced induction | Roots Supercharger; Twin-Scroll Turbo | Horsepower increase plus a grip adjustment; each is archetype-restricted. |
| Exhaust | Free-Flow Exhaust | Horsepower increase from reduced restriction. |
| Wheels | Forged Wheel Package | Visual fitment only; no unearned performance claim. |
| Finish | Midnight Paint Finish | Cosmetic finish only; no unearned performance claim. |

Every installation is checked against the selected vehicle archetype and the item’s compatibility list. The dyno reports the resulting built horsepower, torque estimate, grip, power-to-weight, and estimated ET without adding an artificial horsepower bonus merely for pressing the dyno button. The garage remains the source of truth for installed parts and the race class derives from the resulting build stats.

The current catalog contains the listed parts above. Any future body kit, suspension component, or additional bolt-on must be added with its own price, compatibility rule, mechanical or cosmetic effect, and verification display before it is sold as a product.
